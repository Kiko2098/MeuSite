Client Portal

My own platform to follow consulting and support clients: hour-based contracts, interventions, projects and licences in one place.

  • Python
  • Flask
  • PostgreSQL

Before

  • Support hours tracked in spreadsheets
  • Clients with no visibility of hours used
  • Support requests scattered across email
  • Licences expiring without warning

After

  • Hours used calculated from interventions
  • Clients follow contracts and projects in the portal
  • Support requests opened directly in the portal
  • Automatic warning before each licence expires

How it works

  1. 1

    Contract

    The contract is created with the contracted hours.

  2. 2

    Interventions

    Each intervention is deducted from the contract.

  3. 3

    Alerts

    Automatic email when a percentage of the hours is reached.

  4. 4

    Follow-up

    Clients see contracts, projects and requests in the portal.
