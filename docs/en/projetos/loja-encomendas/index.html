Ordering Shop connected to Primavera

Internal online shop for a food distribution warehouse: every order goes straight into the ERP as a sales document.

  • Python
  • Cegid Primavera
  • Web API

Before

  • Orders recorded outside the invoicing system
  • Item and price lists maintained by hand
  • Every order re-entered manually into the ERP
  • Little control over batches and palletisation

After

  • Catalogue, prices and customers always match the ERP
  • Orders put together on the floor in a few steps
  • Sales document created automatically, ready to invoice
  • Batches, packaging and pallets chosen in the order itself

How it works

  1. 1

    Customer

    Choose the customer, with data coming straight from the ERP.

  2. 2

    Items

    Item, batch and quantity, with support for palletisation and packaging.

  3. 3

    Confirmation

    Delivery details and notes; the order is kept in the history.

  4. 4

    Invoicing

    The order enters the ERP as a sales document, ready to be finalised.

Highlights

Always up-to-date catalogue

Items, prices and customers read from the ERP, with no parallel lists.

Order history

Each order keeps its status, delivery details and notes.

User management

Own login, to control who creates and views orders.

Document printing

Built into the order confirmation flow.
