Extensions for Cegid Primavera V10

C# extensions for Cegid Primavera V10 that add business rules and custom screens without changing the base product.

  • C#
  • .NET
  • Cegid Primavera

Before

  • Business rules relying on whoever is invoicing
  • Credit limits checked by hand
  • Prices that didn't reflect each batch's cost
  • Mistakes only caught after the document was issued

After

  • Validations when the document is created
  • Credit limits and dates checked automatically
  • Price suggested from the chosen batch
  • POS and screens adapted to the company's daily work

How it's done

  1. 1

    Analysis

    Identify the rules and where in the ERP they should apply.

  2. 2

    Development

    C# extension on the editor and POS events.

  3. 3

    Delivery

    A single DLL, with no external dependencies.

  4. 4

    Installation

    Installed on every workstation and POS.

Highlights

Rules in documents

Customer credit limit, blocking future dates and batch control.

Price per batch

The price reflects the real cost of each goods receipt.

Custom POS

Visual item catalogue at the point of sale, designed for fast service.

Custom Web API endpoints

Such as the monthly SAF-T export, reusing the already authenticated engine.
