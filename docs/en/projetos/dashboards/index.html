Management Dashboards on Primavera

Day-to-day indicators read directly from the ERP database: sales, pending orders and stock on your phone.

  • Python
  • SQL Server
  • Cegid Primavera

Before

  • Management information only available inside the ERP
  • Reports run and exported to Excel
  • No quick view on the phone
  • Pending orders followed up late

After

  • Sales, pending orders and stock in real time
  • No manual exports
  • Available on phone, computer and TVs
  • Secure access, with no VPN or open ports

How it works

  1. 1

    Data

    Direct queries to the ERP database.

  2. 2

    Indicators

    Sales by period, customer and item, pending orders and stock.

  3. 3

    Access

    Published with authentication and a PIN, through Cloudflare Tunnel.

  4. 4

    Reports

    The same data feeds PDF reports sent by email.
