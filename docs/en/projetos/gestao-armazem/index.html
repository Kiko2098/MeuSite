Warehouse Management with Android and Primavera

Android app for rugged phones with barcode scanners, connected directly to the ERP: order checking, loading and deliveries without paper.

  • Python
  • Android
  • Cegid Primavera
  • MDM

Before

  • Order checking and loading recorded on paper
  • Data re-entered by hand into the ERP
  • Picking errors only caught at the customer
  • No visibility of what had already left the warehouse

After

  • Every box is scanned and validated on the spot
  • Documents checked directly in the ERP, with no re-entry
  • Invoice generated automatically at the end of checking
  • Order status visible in real time, including on the warehouse TVs

How it works

  1. 1

    Sign in

    Each person signs in by scanning their QR card; their role defines what they can do.

  2. 2

    Check

    Orders, transfers and losses are checked by scanning the QR labels on the boxes.

  3. 3

    Load

    The driver assigns invoices to the vehicle, validating the number of boxes.

  4. 4

    Deliver

    Delivery to the customer or recording of returns, with the document generated automatically.

Highlights

Labels and receipts

Direct printing to Zebra printers (ZPL) and ESC/POS receipts, with no drivers or print dialogs.

Managed devices

Phones in kiosk mode, configured remotely through MDM, with no secrets in the app code.

Direct ERP connection

Documents are read and written in the ERP through a dedicated API, with no exports or intermediate files.

Web packing station

Orders are picked at a web station in the warehouse, which feeds the checking on the phone.
